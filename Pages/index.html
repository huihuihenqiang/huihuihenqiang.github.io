I study how language-model agents learn from experience, use external knowledge, and make reliable decisions over extended interactions.

My recent work connects clinical-agent evaluation, regression-safe knowledge routing, audits of LLM judges, and exact feedback reduction for model comparison. Drawing on statistics, I aim to develop agents that improve over time and evaluation methods that make their progress measurable, trustworthy, and efficient.
